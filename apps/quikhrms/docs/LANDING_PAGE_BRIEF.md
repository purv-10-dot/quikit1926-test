# Quikit HRMS — Landing Page Brief (for UX/UI)

> Hand-off document for designing the **marketing landing page**. It describes the product, audience, messaging, page structure, visual direction, and the assets/copy to produce. Items marked **[FILL]** need input from marketing/founders (pricing, logos, testimonials, real metrics).

---

## 1. Product in one line
**Quikit HRMS** — an all-in-one, multi-tenant HR platform that runs the entire employee lifecycle: hiring, onboarding, attendance, leave, payroll, performance, and compliance — built for Indian businesses.

**Elevator pitch (2 sentences):**
Quikit HRMS replaces the spreadsheets and disconnected tools HR teams juggle with one modern system — from posting a job to running statutory-compliant payroll. AI-assisted, India-ready (EPF, ESI, PT, TDS, Form 24Q/16), and fast.

---

## 2. Who it's for (audience)
- **Primary:** HR managers / HR admins at Indian SMBs and mid-market companies (50–1000 employees).
- **Secondary:** Founders/Ops at growing startups; Finance teams (payroll & TDS); People/Talent teams (hiring).
- **End users:** Every employee (self-service: attendance, leave, payslips, documents).

**Personas to speak to on the page:**
1. **HR Admin** — "I want one system instead of five." (payroll, compliance, onboarding)
2. **Manager** — "Approve leave, see my team's attendance & roster." 
3. **Employee** — "Check in, apply leave, download payslip — from anywhere."

---

## 3. Core value propositions (the "why")
1. **One platform, whole lifecycle** — hire → onboard → attendance → payroll → performance → exit.
2. **India-ready compliance, built in** — EPF, ESI, PT, LWF, Bonus, TDS, **Form 24Q & Form 16**, Form 12BB declarations. No bolt-ons.
3. **Payroll you can trust** — salary templates, statutory auto-calculation, pay runs, payslips, bank advice, reconciliation.
4. **AI-assisted HR** — resume/ATS scoring, document chat, policy extraction, bulk imports.
5. **Multi-tenant SaaS** — secure, role-based access with org hierarchy; each company's data isolated.
6. **Self-service for everyone** — employees and managers act directly; HR stops being a ticket queue.

---

## 4. Feature highlights (group these into page sections)

**Hire & Onboard**
- Requisitions, candidate pipeline, interviews, offers
- Candidate document collection, ATS scoring
- Structured onboarding (and offboarding)

**Time & Attendance**
- Web/mobile check-in/out, regularization with approvals
- Shifts, **Duty Roster** (per-day shift & week-off planning, publish)
- Timesheets, projects & job-based time tracking

**Leave Management**
- Configurable leave types & policies, balances, calendar
- Pro-rata allocation for new joiners, leave approvals

**Payroll & Compliance** *(headline differentiator for India)*
- Salary templates with auto-balancing components
- Statutory: EPF, ESI, Professional Tax, LWF, Bonus
- Pay runs, payslips, bank advice, reconciliation
- **TDS, Form 24Q, Form 16**, Form 12BB, reimbursement claims & investment declarations

**Performance & Engagement**
- Goals, appraisal cycles, manager reviews, continuous feedback, PIPs
- Announcements, recognition, surveys

**Documents, Assets & Reports**
- Company document templates + employee vault
- Asset assignment/tracking
- Dashboards, analytics, and exportable reports

**Platform**
- Role-based access control with org hierarchy (multi-role)
- Multi-tenant, secure JWT auth, audit logs
- AI chat & document assistant

## 7. Visual direction (match the existing product UI)
Keep the landing page consistent with the in-app look so the product feels like one brand.

- **Palette (from the app):**
  - Deep navy (primary/dark): `#16243A` (and `#1E3354`)
  - Brand blue (accent/CTAs): `#3b82f6` → `#2563eb` (gradient used in heroes)
  - Light blue tint (surfaces): `#dbeafe`
  - Success/positive: emerald `#10b981` family
  - Neutrals: white surfaces, gray-50/100 backgrounds, gray-900 text
- **Type:** App uses a **serif display** font for big headings + a clean sans for body. Mirror that contrast (elegant serif H1/H2, modern sans for UI/body). **[FILL exact fonts]**
- **Feel:** clean, modern SaaS, lots of whitespace, soft rounded cards (`rounded-lg/xl`), subtle shadows, gradient navy→blue accents. Professional but not stiff.
- **Imagery:** real product screenshots (dashboard, payslip, duty-roster grid, pay-run, leave calendar) in device/browser frames; simple line icons (lucide-react is used in-app — match that icon style).

## 10. Tone
Confident, clear, helpful. Lead with outcomes ("run payroll in minutes", "compliance handled"), not jargon. Indian-business-aware. Avoid generic "AI-powered everything" hype — show specific, believable AI uses.

---

### Notes for the designer
- **Payroll + India compliance is the strongest differentiator** — give it the most visual weight.
- The product is genuinely broad; don't try to show every module in the hero. Lead with the lifecycle story, then let module sections do the detail.
- Everything marked **[FILL]** (pricing, logos, testimonials, real numbers, exact fonts, company/legal) must come from the business before final copy.
