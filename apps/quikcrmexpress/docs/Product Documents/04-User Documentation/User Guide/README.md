# QuikCRM — User Guide

Welcome to QuikCRM, your sales workspace for capturing leads, working them through a pipeline, and converting them into customers.

This guide is written for people who use the product day to day. It explains what each feature does, when to use it, exactly how to use it, and what to expect afterwards.

> **A note on what you can see.** QuikCRM shows you different things depending on your role and how your organisation is configured. If a menu item described in this guide is not on your screen, it is almost certainly a permissions matter, not a fault. Start with **[Roles and visibility](roles-and-visibility.md)**.

## Modules

### [Leads](leads/README.md)
The core of QuikCRM. Capture prospects, filter and organise them, work them through your pipeline, and convert them into customers.

[Lead list](leads/lead-list.md) · [Advanced filters](leads/advanced-filters.md) · [Saved views and lists](leads/saved-views.md) · [Selection and bulk actions](leads/bulk-actions.md) · [Create a lead](leads/create-lead.md) · [Lead details](leads/lead-details.md) · [Convert a lead](leads/convert-lead.md) · [Kanban board](leads/kanban.md) · [Import](leads/import.md) · [Export](leads/export.md) · [Trash](leads/trash.md)

### [Accounts](accounts.md)
The companies you sell to. Each account gathers its contacts, leads, opportunities, tasks and history in one place.

### [Contacts](contacts.md)
The individual people you deal with, usually attached to an account.

### [Opportunities](opportunities.md)
Active deals with a value, a close date and a pipeline stage. Managed on a drag-and-drop board or as a list.

### [Activities](activities.md)
Your record of every call, email, meeting and note. Log activity from anywhere in the product.

### [Tasks](tasks.md)
Your to-do list, with List, Week and My Day views.

### [Telephony](telephony/README.md)
Click-to-call from any record, with a guided outcome form after every call.
[Making calls](telephony/README.md) · [Recording a call outcome](telephony/call-disposition.md)

### [Dashboard](dashboard.md)
Your daily starting point: pipeline value, work due today, and trend charts.

## Conventions used in this guide

| Marker | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Important** | Behaviour you should understand *before* using the feature |
| **Gotcha** | Real behaviour that commonly surprises people |
| **Permissions** | Who can use the feature |
| **Prerequisites** | What must exist or be configured first |

Navigation paths are written as **Sidebar → Leads → Advanced**.
